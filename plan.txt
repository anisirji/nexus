Updated Phase 1 Development Plan
AI + Google Workspace Integration (Enhanced)

Project Overview
Build an AI assistant that deeply integrates with Google Workspace - handling Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Tasks, Forms, and Meet. Users log in with Google, grant permissions, then use natural language to automate complex workflows like scheduling interviews with automated emails, forms, and follow-ups.
